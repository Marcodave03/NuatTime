Rest is
a practice.

Thai reflexology works the body’s energy lines to settle the nervous system and bring you back to a slower pace.

We opened on 11 December 2023 in Ruko Golf Island, Pantai Indah Kapuk, and have kept the same intent since: an unhurried room, trained hands, and treatments that leave you looser than you arrived.

Walk in, or send us a message on WhatsApp and we will hold a bed for you.

Open every day 09:00 – 21:00 Since December 2023 Where Golf Island, PIK

What we practise

Royal Orchid

The Thai lineage our therapists are trained in, kept close to how it is taught at home.

Mindfulness

A quiet room, no rush between sessions, and full attention on the person on the bed.

Touch

Pressure read from the body itself, adjusted as we go, never applied to a formula.

Treatments

Four ways to be worked on. Tell us where you hold tension and we will start there.

Full Body Reflexology

Pressure applied along the body to release tension and improve circulation. Feet are worked with cream; everywhere else, dry.

Oil Massage

Warm oil worked into the body to ease tension and draw you down into deep relaxation.

Thai Massage

Dry massage in the Thai technique — stretching and pressing to loosen stiff muscle and give movement back.

Kop & Kerik

Traditional cupping and coin therapy, taken on their own or added to any treatment above.

Rates

Priced by the hour. No membership, no package to sign up for.
